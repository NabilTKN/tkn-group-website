  • Industrial cleaning

Prepared in advance. Followed up on site.

A job is prepared with the people who know the site. We clarify the requirements, access and schedule before bringing in the team.

  1. Define the scope

    We discuss the installations involved, the frequency you need and the constraints of the site. A site visit is arranged when the nature of the job requires it.

  2. Prepare the job

    Hours, access and rules are agreed with your teams. Equipment and supervision are matched to the planned work.

  3. Follow the work through

    On site, the team leader organises the work. Your point of contact follows the job and any adjustments that may be needed.

Very different needs on the ground

Continuous upkeep, night-time cleaning or work during a production shutdown: the organisation depends on how each site operates.

These examples are presented without identifying the clients or their sites.

  • Steel industry

    Upkeep across every shift

    At a steelworks, the upkeep of the changing rooms and washrooms is organised around the clock. Day and night work on the industrial equipment completes the service.

  • Concrete production

    Cleaning before the next cycle

    Cleaning the machines at night removes residue before it hardens and gets the installations ready for the next production run.

  • Shutdowns

    Working within the planned window

    The deep cleaning of the installations is prepared with the site’s teams and carried out to the production shutdown schedule.

What matters during the job

  • Clearly identified supervision

    A team leader organises the work on site. Managers follow the job and stay in touch with your teams.

  • A schedule that suits your site

    We agree working slots with you, taking into account your hours and your shutdown periods.

  • Rules built into the preparation

    Access conditions, protective equipment and the necessary qualifications are taken into account before the job starts.

  • A point of contact for what comes next

    Your contact follows the service and works out with you any changes needed as your requirements evolve.

Let’s talk about your site

A regular service, work to schedule or a one-off job? Tell us what you need, where the work will take place and your intended timescale. We will then discuss the details with you to define the service.
