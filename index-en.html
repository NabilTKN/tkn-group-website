Expertise for your installations and buildings.

Industrial cleaning, building maintenance and renovation, damage restoration and grounds maintenance: TKN Technics supports your projects with qualified teams and supervisors who are there on site.

See what we do Contact us

What we do

  • Industrial cleaning 

    Cleaning of machines, equipment and production installations, under a regular contract or as a one-off job, including during shutdowns. Our teams work to each site's hours and safety rules.

  • Building maintenance and renovation 

    Roofing, plumbing, heating, electrical work and finishing: we maintain, repair and renovate your buildings. We coordinate the trades and oversee the work from quote to handover.

  • Damage restoration and clearance 

    After water damage or a fire, we clean, dry out and restore the property. We also clear out premises, cellars and warehouses, and take away bulky items.

  • Grounds maintenance 

    Mowing, pruning, tree surgery, felling and scrub clearance: we maintain outdoor areas and the grounds around your buildings, through scheduled visits or one-off jobs.

Experience in demanding environments

Steel, concrete production, paper, carbochemicals, logistics and nuclear: our teams work on sites where preparation, safety and keeping to schedule are part of the daily routine.

For confidentiality reasons, we do not name our clients or their sites.

  • Steel industry

    Round-the-clock site upkeep

    Upkeep of changing rooms and washrooms with 24/7 cover, alongside day and night work on the industrial equipment.

  • Concrete production

    Night-time machine cleaning

    Every night, the machines are cleaned to remove residue before it hardens and to get them ready for the next production run.

  • Shutdowns

    Cleaning during a production shutdown

    Deep cleaning of the installations during the shutdown period, to a schedule agreed with the site's teams.

Our commitments

From regular contracts to one-off jobs, we commit to quality work, punctuality and reliable follow-up.

  • Quality of work

    Our teams work under team leaders and qualified managers, who are present on site to oversee the work as it is carried out.

  • Punctuality

    We work at the agreed times, taking your operational constraints into account, by day or by night when your site requires it.

  • Safety

    As a VCA*-certified company, we assign every job to staff who hold the required qualifications and follow the rules specific to your site.

  • Follow-up

    Your account manager works out your requirements with you, adjusts the service when your constraints change and remains your contact for the full term of the contract.
