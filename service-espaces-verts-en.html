Maintenance of your grounds and surrounding areas

Business sites, residential buildings, public spaces and gardens: we maintain outdoor areas through regular visits or for one-off jobs. The work programme takes into account the season, the vegetation and how the site is used.

Request a quote +32 499 31 25 23

Upkeep through the seasons

Mowing grassed areas, trimming hedges and shrubs, upkeep of the surrounding areas: we agree the services and the schedule of visits with you.

Tree work

Pruning, tree surgery, felling and stump removal are assessed according to the layout of the land and how close the trees are to buildings, roads or utility networks. The work is prepared taking into account access and any permits that may be required.

Scrub clearance and land restoration

Embankments, plots before building work or overgrown surroundings: we clear the agreed areas to make them easier to access and maintain. How branches are dealt with and how green waste is removed are specified as part of the service.
