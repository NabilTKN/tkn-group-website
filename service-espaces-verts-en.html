Grounds maintenance

We maintain outdoor areas and the grounds around buildings: mowing, pruning, tree surgery, felling, stump removal and scrub clearance. We work for businesses, managing agents, local authorities and private individuals, through scheduled visits or one-off jobs.

Contact us +32 499 31 25 23

Our services

Upkeep

Mowing lawns and grassed areas, trimming hedges and shrubs, at business sites, residential buildings and public spaces.

Pruning and tree surgery

Pruning trees and cutting back branches that obstruct access routes, buildings, roofs or utility lines.

Felling and stump removal

Near a building or a road, the tree is taken down in sections to control the fall of each piece. The stump can then be removed.

Scrub clearance and land restoration

Neglected land, embankments, the surroundings of industrial sites, plots before building work: we clear the vegetation so that the land can be accessed and maintained.

Scheduled visits

For mowing and pruning, we agree a schedule of visits with you to suit the season and how the site is used.

One-off jobs

Land to clear before building work, a tree too close to a roof, a hedge that needs cutting back: we also come out for a single visit, on a quoted basis. Depending on what is agreed, branches are chipped on site and green waste is taken away.

Grounds that need maintaining?

Send us the address, the approximate area and the type of work. Photos help us prepare the quote.
