Teams on the ground, with supervisors on site

Our staff work in industrial cleaning, building work, damage restoration and grounds maintenance. The work is organised on site by a team leader, supported by managers who follow the jobs and contracts.

Knowing the trade and the site

Preparation takes into account the techniques to be used, the installations and the access rules. On sites where we work regularly, the teams also get to know the premises and the people they deal with.

Organising the work day to day

Allocating tasks, rules, equipment and following up the service: supervisors provide the link between the client’s needs and the work carried out on the ground.

Preparing the working conditions

Skills, qualifications and protective equipment are checked according to the planned job and the requirements of the site.
