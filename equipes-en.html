Our teams

Our staff are organised by trade: industrial cleaning, building work, damage restoration and grounds maintenance. On every job, they work under a team leader, backed by qualified managers.

Contact us Apply to join TKN Technics

Hands-on skills

Each team knows the techniques and equipment of its trade. On sites where we work regularly, it also knows the installations, access points, rules and contacts, which makes it easier to get each job under way.

On-site supervision

The team leader organises the work on site and makes sure the result is right. Managers oversee jobs and contracts, while our account managers stay in touch with the client about any request.

Safety and training

The company is VCA* certified. We assign every job to staff who hold the required qualifications, and our teams follow the safety rules specific to each site, with the appropriate protective equipment.
