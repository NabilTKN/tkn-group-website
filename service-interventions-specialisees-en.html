Damage restoration and clearance

After water damage or a fire, we clean, dry out and restore the property. We also clear out homes, cellars and warehouses, and take away furniture, materials and bulky items. We work for businesses, managing agents, property managers and private individuals.

Cleaning and restoration after water damage or fire

Once you have called us, we go through the situation with you: the type of damage, the rooms affected, access and your insurance claim. Following this conversation, we set the date of the work and agree what needs to be done.

Water damage

Pumping out and extracting the water, stripping out damaged materials, then drying the walls, floors and ceilings, checked with moisture readings.

Fire

Cleaning soot from walls, ceilings and salvageable furniture, treating smoke odours, stripping out and removing burnt materials.

Cleaning the property

Once the water or soot has been removed, the premises and any salvageable contents are cleaned before restoration begins.

Restoration

Plastering, painting, flooring, electrical work, plumbing: the work is carried out by our Building maintenance and renovation service, with the same point of contact.

For your insurance claim

We photograph the damage before starting work and document the work carried out. These records can be attached to your claim or passed on to the loss adjuster. The decision on whether the claim is covered rests with your insurer.

Property clearance

Clearing out premises

We clear out homes, cellars, warehouses and cluttered storage areas ahead of a sale, a new tenancy, building work or the resumption of business. Waste is sorted on site by category, then loaded into skips placed for the job. The property is then swept and cleaned.

Removal of bulky items

Furniture, materials, construction waste, bulky items: we load them and take them away, in our own vehicles or in skips depending on the volume.

Asbestos and hazardous waste must be handled by specialist contractors. Please tell us in your request if either is present.

Damaged premises or a property to clear?

Tell us about the property, the situation and your deadline. Photos and, for fire or water damage, your insurance claim reference help us assess your request.
