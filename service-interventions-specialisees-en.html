Damage restoration and property clearance

Water damage, a fire or premises to clear out: we assess the condition of the property and organise the necessary operations. Cleaning, drying out, removal and restoration work are defined according to the situation.

Request a quote +32 499 31 25 23
  • Damage restoration 

    Water damage or fire: cleaning, drying out and restoration of the property.

  • Clearing out premises 

    Homes, cellars, warehouses: sorting, removal and clean-up.

After water damage or a fire

We go over the damage, access and the operations required with you. Work begins once access and safety conditions allow.

Water damage

Pumping out and extracting the water, stripping out damaged materials and drying out the affected areas.

Fire

Soot cleaning, odour treatment and removal of damaged materials, depending on their nature and the working conditions.

Restoration

Once the premises are ready, plastering, painting, flooring and building services work is organised with our maintenance and renovation service.

Useful information for your claim

Photos and details of the work carried out can support your insurance claim. Whether the cost is covered depends on your policy and on the insurer’s decision.

Clearing out premises and removing bulky items

Homes, cellars, warehouses and storage areas: we organise the sorting, loading and removal of the contents set out in the quote. A clean-up can follow the clearance.

Please tell us before any work starts if asbestos or hazardous waste is known to be present. These require specialist handling.

Tell us about the situation

Let us know the location, the type of damage or the volume to be removed, and your preferred deadline. Only include photos that are useful for assessing your request.
