Cleaning after damage

After an incident, every day counts: moisture encourages mould, while soot and smoke keep attacking surfaces. We step in to stop this deterioration and make the premises healthy again before the works. Repairs can then be carried out by our general maintenance service.

Water damage

Pumping out standing water, extraction, surface cleaning and controlled drying checked with moisture readings.

Pumps, wet vacuums, dehumidifiers, air movers, moisture meters.

Fire

Soot cleaning on walls and ceilings, cleaning of furniture and salvageable contents, smoke odour treatment, removal of burnt materials.

Industrial vacuums, high-pressure and steam cleaners.

Mould and disinfection

Mould treatment on affected surfaces and disinfection of premises after flooding, sewage backflow or unsanitary conditions.

Emptying and clearance

We empty homes, cellars, warehouses and cluttered storage areas, ahead of a sale, a let, building work or a restart of activity. Waste is sorted on site by category, wood, metal, rubble and bulky items, and loaded into containers or skips placed for the job. The premises are then handed back empty, swept and cleaned.

Removal of waste and bulky items

Furniture, materials, construction waste or bulky items: we load and remove them with our own vehicles or by skip, depending on the volume.

Who we work for

Insurers and loss adjusters, managing agents and property managers, businesses and private individuals.

A situation to deal with?

Describe the premises, the situation and the deadline. Photos help us assess the volume and reply faster.
