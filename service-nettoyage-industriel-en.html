Industrial cleaning of your installations

Machines, equipment and production areas: we clean your installations under regular contracts, as one-off jobs or during shutdowns. The scope and hours are set with your teams, according to the constraints of the site.

Request a quote +32 499 31 25 23

From production equipment to the grounds around the site

Machines and production lines

Degreasing and removal of encrusted dirt and deposits from machines, conveyors and structures. Work on equipment is carried out while it is stopped, under the safety conditions agreed with the site.

Tanks, hoppers, silos and pits

Cleaning of internal walls, removal of deposits, sludge vacuuming and clearing of pits, bunds and drainage channels, depending on how the installations are configured.

Ducts, shafts and hoods

Dust removal and degreasing of the ventilation and extraction systems included in the service.

Workshops and production buildings

Cleaning of floors, cladding, glazing and other surfaces within the agreed scope of work.

Staff facilities

Upkeep of the changing rooms, showers and washrooms used in the day-to-day running of the site.

Site exteriors

Cleaning of loading bays, roads, car parks and surrounding areas, as well as clean-up after works.

Three ways of working

A regular contract

The frequency, the areas to be cleaned and the hours are set with you. The team assigned to the site gets to know the installations, access points and rules, so that the service fits into the day-to-day running of your business.

A one-off need

A heavily fouled installation, a deep clean or a clean-up after works: we assess the situation and prepare a service that matches the need identified.

A shutdown

We prepare the cleaning with your teams ahead of the shutdown: access, areas involved, sequence of operations and available time slots. The work then fits into the agreed schedule.

Methods and preparation

High pressure, steam, industrial vacuuming or pit clearing: the methods are chosen according to the deposits to be removed and the characteristics of the installations. Work at height or in confined spaces is specifically prepared and assigned to operatives who hold the required skills and qualifications. Our teams work in sectors including the steel industry, concrete production, paper, carbochemicals and logistics, as well as on a nuclear site. Each site’s access and scheduling constraints are built in from the preparation stage.
