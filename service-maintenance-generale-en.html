Building maintenance and renovation

We maintain, repair, renovate and restore buildings for businesses, managing agents and private individuals: roofing, plumbing, heating, electrical work and finishing. A single point of contact organises the work and follows its progress through to handover.

Contact us +32 499 31 25 23

Maintenance and repairs

A leak, a heating breakdown, an electrical fault, water getting in through the roof: we track down the cause and carry out the repair, in commercial buildings and homes alike.

Renovation and restoration

Offices, shops, communal areas and homes: partitions and plastering, painting, flooring, electrical work and plumbing. We work in both empty and occupied premises; in occupied premises, work areas are cordoned off and the schedule is drawn up with the occupants.

After water damage or a fire, we carry out the rebuilding work once the property has been dried out and cleaned. If the damage has only just happened, see our Damage restoration and clearance page.

Trades

Roofing and waterproofing

Repairs to leaks, gutters and box gutters, waterproofing work and re-roofing.

Heating

Boiler breakdown call-outs and repairs, replacement of heating system components.

Electrical

Breakdown call-outs, equipment replacement and electrical work in buildings.

Plumbing and sanitaryware

Leak detection and repair, pipework, replacement of sanitaryware.

Plastering and partitions

Repairing and building walls, partitions and ceilings.

Painting and floor coverings

Walls, ceilings and woodwork; laying and repair of tiles and other floor coverings.

Coordinating and overseeing the work

Our technicians carry out the work and, depending on the trades involved, we bring in partner companies. We plan the sequence of work, monitor progress on site and carry out the handover with you.

A building that needs maintenance or renovation?

Send us the address, the type of work and, if you have any, a few photos. We will get back to you to go over the details of your request.
