Who is the controller?

The controller is TKN Group SRL, Avenue Paul De Merten 71, 1090 Jette, Belgium, enterprise number 0746.960.475.

For any question about your data or to exercise your rights, you can write to info@tkn-technics.be or contact us by post at this address.

What data do we use and why?

Contact and quote requests

When you contact us, we use the contact details and information you provide to understand your request, respond to it and, where appropriate, prepare an offer. This may include your name, your business contact details, your company, the location of the work and any information relevant to the service.

When you request a service for yourself, the legal basis for this processing is the pre-contractual steps you ask us to take. When you represent a business, it is based on our legitimate interest in managing our business communications, with due regard for your rights.

Job applications

When you apply, we process the information you send us: identity, contact details, role applied for, experience, skills, availability, message and any professional documents attached. We use it to review your application and get back to you. The legal basis for this processing is the pre-contractual steps taken at your request.

The data requested is limited to what is needed to review your application. Please do not send any identity document, national register number or medical data through this form.

Website operation and security

When you visit the website, technical data may be processed by the services required for its hosting and security, in particular [categories to be confirmed with the hosting provider: IP address, date and time, page requested, technical browser information]. This processing is used to provide the website and to prevent or investigate incidents. Its legal basis is the legitimate interest in ensuring the operation and protection of the service.

What are your rights?

Under the conditions laid down by the GDPR, you can request access to your data, its rectification or erasure, or the restriction of its processing. You can also object to certain processing based on a legitimate interest and request data portability where this right applies.

Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

To exercise your rights, write to info@tkn-technics.be, stating your request. We will respond without undue delay and, in principle, within one month of receiving it. If a permitted extension is necessary because of the complexity or number of requests, we will inform you within that first month, together with the reasons for the delay. If we have reasonable doubts about your identity, we may request the additional information strictly necessary to verify it.

How can you lodge a complaint?

You can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority. Information and the procedure to follow are available on its website: www.dataprotectionauthority.be. This right is without prejudice to any other remedies available to you.

Automated decisions

As part of the processing described here, we do not take any decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal effects concerning you or affects you in a similar way.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not set cookies and does not use other trackers, such as browser local storage, pixels or audience measurement tools. The fonts and other resources of the website are served by the website itself, with no calls to third-party services while you browse.

These technologies are distinct from the technical logs described above and from the information you choose to provide, for example when you send an application.
